## 1. WHAT THE HELL IS THIS

Congratulations, human. You thought you unboxed a cheap $15 **M5StickS3 (ESP32-S3)** microcontroller development board?

Unfortunately, the Life Foundation concealed the truth in the factory firmware—this is a reinforced micro-electronic containment canister, designation **LF-978**. Sealed beneath the glass lives a hyper-reactive liquid organism from planet Klyntar: **Venom**.

This is not a toy playing canned looping GIFs. It is an autonomous silicon-based synthetic lifeform powered by real viscous fluid dynamics, a soft-body biomechanical spine, an insatiable predatory drive, and an asynchronous dual-core neural mind.

### Hardcore Technical Highlights (for tech freaks who won't listen):
- **Metaball Scalar Field & Fluid Dynamics**: 5-node asymmetric biomechanical chain with screen-edge boundary field enhancement. When resting on the ground, it flattens naturally like viscous gel, never turning into a sharp flying-saucer wafer;
- **FreeRTOS Dual-Core Heterogeneous Consciousness**:
  - **Core 1**: Renders fluid metaball fields, spring constraints, eyeball gaze-tracking, and cellular Voronoi skin at rock-solid 30 FPS;
  - **Core 0**: Silently handles SSL handshakes, HTTP sockets, and LLM neural emergence with zero frame drops and zero UI stutter;
- **Full Physical Sensor Immersion**: 6-axis IMU gravity simulation, ultra-sensitive acoustic stimulation, and micro-motor haptic feedback.

## 2. SYMBIOTE MODES (What the slime does behind glass)

Venom is not your obedient pet. It has its own metabolic cycle, stamina, and questionable pastimes. Its behavioral state transitions follow a momentum-conserving Biological Behavioral Graph:

- **👁️ Border Reconnaissance (`STATE_PEEK`)**:
  When curiosity wars with vigilance, Venom submerges its entire mass deep beyond the screen bezel, leaving **only the crown of its head and white eye-spots visible**. It peeks cautiously; any disturbance sends it retreating into the dark. Upon exit, it glides out along the rim—no erratic jump cuts.
- **🐛 Filament Ground Creep (`STATE_CREEP`)**:
  A compact, bulbous body crawls along the screen floor (or walls). Its base flattens flush against the surface while tiny foot filaments paddle forward. Zero jellyfish-style pulsating convulsions.
- **🧗 Large-Stride Tentacle Crawl (`STATE_CRAWL`)**:
  When yearning for the ceiling or opposite borders, its head bursts forward with three-pronged grappling tentacles, dragging its entire mass across the display in rapid, heavy strides.
- **🕸️ Ceiling Web-Swing (`STATE_SWING`)**:
  Upon reaching the ceiling, Venom fires silk anchors into the top glass, hanging down in a pendulum state. Tilting the device shifts gravitational sway, while it autonomously kicks its legs to pump swing momentum!
- **🚀 Slime Trampoline (`STATE_BOUNCE`)**:
  On flat ground, it squashes itself flat to store kinetic tension, then launches like a rocket into the upper atmosphere with gelatinous tremors, absorbing energy on touchdown for another bounce.
- **🌀 Soft-Body Ball Roll (`STATE_ROLL`)**:
  When hitting the deck with horizontal velocity, it tucks its entire anatomy into a dense spherical marble, rolls rapidly across the glass floor, and rebounds off side walls.
- **⚽ Autonomous Ball Juggling (`STATE_BALL_PLAY`)**:
  Buds off an independent symbiote droplet from its tail and joyfully juggles it in mid-air using headbutts and lateral tentacles. When done, a tentacle snatches the ball back, reabsorbing it into its core with a burst of pink hearts.
- **🐾 Dust-Bouncing Predator (`STATE_CATCH_DUST`)**:
  Hunkers low into ambush stance, lunges at airborne dust motes at lightning speed (155 px/s), slams into the deck, then looks up bewildered, spitting out an ink question mark `?`.
- **🦇 Ceiling Bat-Hang (`STATE_BAT_HANG`)**:
  Suspends itself upside down from the ceiling rafters to zone out or take a quick nap.
- **💤 Deep Hibernation & Recovery (`STATE_SLEEP`)**:
  When energy is depleted (`Energy < 0.15`) and left undisturbed, it coils tightly into a quiet corner with eyes shut. Every 5.5 seconds, a trail of rising `Zz` bubbles wafts from its snout during a 10–15 minute restorative sleep cycle.

> **Prey Reward**: Devouring a bug instantly replenishes its vitality (`Energy +0.30`), accompanied by micro-droplet splashes and a crisp haptic purr.

## 10. TROUBLESHOOTING (The Confessional)

- **Q: Why is it hiding on the border, peeking and refusing to come out?**  
  **A**: It's suspicious of you. Give it 2–3 seconds or hold still. Once it finishes scanning, it will glide along the rim or extend tentacles into the center. Don't tap the glass unless you want to provoke it.
- **Q: When I flick the device, why does it go "splat" and stick to the edge like mud?**  
  **A**: This is intentional Sticky Toy Physics! When whipped into the wall, its momentum is absorbed by the boundary, flattening it into a pancake against the glass. It will peel itself off shortly.
- **Q: Why does pressing BtnA cause it to jolt violently and spray droplets? Did I break it?**  
  **A**: That's neural irritation. You prodded an extraterrestrial symbiote with an electrode. The fact that it didn't digest your finger through the glass is sheer ESP32 architecture mercy.
- **Q: It closed its eyes, stopped moving, and bubbles with 'Zz' are floating up. Is it dead?**  
  **A**: It burned through all its energy and entered `STATE_SLEEP`. It requires a 10–15 minute deep slumber to recharge. If impatient, blow hard into the mic or shake the device to wake it.
- **Q: Why doesn't it balloon and oscillate like a jellyfish during stealth and creeping anymore?**  
  **A**: Because people complained it was flabbier than jellyfish jelly. Venom is now a dense, taut, muscular fluid slime that naturally flattens against surfaces and refuses to be a medusa.
